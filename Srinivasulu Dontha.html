Dual-Camera AI Proctoring System

May – Jul 2026

An MVP for fairer online exams. The student's laptop webcam covers face and upper body, and a phone placed beside them covers the screen, keyboard, hands, and desk. The exam starts only after both cameras connect and a setup checklist passes.

  1. ConnectThe phone joins by QR code. WebRTC video is signaled through Socket.IO.
  2. CheckA pre-exam angle checklist must pass before the exam unlocks.
  3. WatchTensorFlow.js COCO-SSD looks for people, phones, books, and laptops in both feeds.
  4. LogViolations get a severity level and an evidence snapshot.
  5. ActSerious violations auto-submit the exam. Admins see live sessions and reports.
